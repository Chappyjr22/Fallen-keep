# The Corrupted King: locked visual identity

Approved source of truth: `public/assets/king-comic.png`, especially the top-right close-up. Follow-up approved scene atlas: `public/assets/ascent-comic.png`. Always supply the original as the image reference for future portraits, cinematics and boss sprites.

- Tall, narrow, jagged gold crown with uneven pointed spires.
- Enclosed blackened skeletal helmet with a skull-like face grille. No exposed human face.
- Bright emerald eyes and green spectral wisps from the helmet and shoulders.
- Heavy black ribbed plate armor, layered shoulders and articulated gauntlets.
- Ragged deep crimson cloak, gold skull clasps and chains across the chest.
- Broad, imposing silhouette; deliberate, regal posture. Scornful, controlled menace.
- Painterly gothic comic artwork for cinematics; translate these same identifying features into the game's pixel-art boss sprite. Do not turn the future boss into a generic giant skeleton.
- Preserve the crown, helmet, green eyes, crimson cloak and gold clasps at gameplay scale. Never substitute red eyes, a human face, silver crown or different cloak color.

First-floor atmosphere: cold slate-blue marble and shadows, muted crimson runners, warm candlelit islands and restrained emerald corruption near the sealed royal stair. Keep combat silhouettes readable.

The new ascent sequence uses an unseen protagonist viewpoint, so it works for every selected character and either party size. Panel order: stair doorway, King close-up, awakened armor, sealed royal stair. Dialogue is in `public/ascent-story.mjs`; do not bake text into artwork.
